## Known limitations

- Semgrep and OpenRewrite are unavailable on Windows; their adapters report
  `UNAVAILABLE` with the real reason and the gate records a tool gap.
- SpotBugs needs compiled classes; without a compile step it reports
  `UNSUPPORTED` instead of pretending to have scanned.
- The `rule` reviewer backend is deterministic local heuristics, labelled as
  such in every report. Cross-model review requires `review.backend: llm`.
- Built-in analyzers use lightweight parsing, not a real AST; this is a
  recall limitation, reported honestly by the golden suite.
